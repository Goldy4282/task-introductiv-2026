# Fantasy RPG UI Kit - Free Sample
StationSupply. 23 elements from the Standard tier of the wood, gold and parchment RPG UI kit, as SVG and PNG at 1x and 2x (69 files): a wide button in five states (normal, hover, pressed, disabled, focus), a square and a close button, a parchment and a dark panel, an inventory slot, checkbox and toggle (on and off), a bar frame with health and mana fills, and six icons (sword, shield, health potion, coin, gem, key).
Full pack: https://stationsupply.itch.io/fantasy-rpg-ui-kit
The pieces are vector files (SVG) written as code with an AI assistant; the PNGs are renders of them. The commercial-use terms are in LICENSE: use them in your games, no standalone redistribution.
